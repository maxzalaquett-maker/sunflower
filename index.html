Sunflower

Compare sun, shade, and possible garden spots.

1. Find your property

Search for an address in North Carolina or South Carolina, or use your current location. Mecklenburg County properties get enhanced parcel lookup and local aerial imagery when available.

Where this works today

Address search: North Carolina and South Carolina.

Enhanced data: Mecklenburg County, NC parcel boundaries and local aerial imagery.

Outside Mecklenburg: You can still map sunlight by drawing garden beds and shade blockers manually.

2. Map your yard

Draw what could shape the light: garden beds, trees, buildings, sheds, organic shapes, and fence lines. Use Cursor to adjust or delete your notes.

3. Watch the light move

Slide through the year and day to see how estimated shadows shift across the yard.

4. Find the best planting spot

This runs a fuller seasonal check across common garden dates.

Results

Draw at least one garden bed and one or more likely shade blockers, then run the estimate.

Honest estimate

Sunflower estimates sunlight using public map data and your edits. Walk the yard and verify sunlight before planting.

This prototype cannot know recent tree removals, fence height, pruning, leaf density, or neighboring changes unless you add them.

Privacy note

Your drawings are stored only in your browser during this session. This prototype does not save your address, location, or yard plan to a database.
